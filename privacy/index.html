Privacy Policy

CalVault is built so your food diary stays with you. This page explains what data the app uses, where it goes and the choices you have.

Effective: November 8, 2026 Applies to: the CalVault app and calvault.app

The short version

No accountWe don't ask for your email, password or a social login.
Stored on your deviceYour meals, photos, weight and profile are saved locally on your phone. We don't keep a copy.

1. Who we are

CalVault ("CalVault", "we", "us") is a nutrition and calorie tracking app for Android, published on Google Play as com.mrfirdavs.calvault. This policy covers the CalVault app and the website at calvault.app.

If you have a question about this policy, email support@calvault.app.

2. Data stored on your device

CalVault has no user accounts and no cloud database. Everything below stays in the app's private storage on your phone. We can't see or access it.

WhatExamplesWhy
ProfileName or nickname, sex, age, height, weight, goal weight, activity levelTo calculate your daily calorie and macro targets and show your BMI
GoalsDaily calories, protein, carbs, fatTo show progress and what's left for the day
Meal logMeal name, meal type, time, calories, macros, nutrients and the meal photoYour food diary, history and charts
Daily activityWater, sleep, steps, active minutes, weight entriesDaily check-ins and the weight progress chart
Fridge suggestionsYour most recent fridge-photo meal ideasSo you can come back to them
App stateWhether setup is finished, Premium status, free photo countTo run the app and apply the free limit

Conversations with the Nutrition AI are kept in memory only while the app is open and are cleared when you close it.

Because this data lives only on your phone, it isn't backed up by us. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it's gone. If your device has its own backup turned on (for example, Android backup to your Google account), that backup is controlled by you and your device provider.

3. AI features

Some features need an AI model running on a server. When you use one of them, the app sends the content below over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our AI service provider. It's processed to generate a reply and sent back to your phone.

FeatureWhat is sent
Meal photo analysisThe meal photo you take or choose
Fridge meal ideasThe photo of your fridge or pantry
Nutrition AI chatYour message and any photo you attach, plus context so the answer fits your day: today's logged meals and their nutrition, your daily goals and what's left, today's activity (water, sleep, steps, active minutes), and your weight, height, age and BMI

We don't send your name, and we don't attach any account ID, advertising ID or contact details to these requests.

We use a third-party AI API (currently cc-vibe), which runs the request on a large language model. We don't store these requests ourselves. The provider handles them under its own terms and privacy policy and may keep them briefly to operate, secure and debug its service. If you don't want a photo or question to leave your device, don't use the AI features for it.

AI results are estimates and can be wrong. You can review and edit a meal before saving it.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

CalVault Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment, and we never see or store your card details, billing address or Google account password. The app asks Google Play which subscriptions are active on your Google account and keeps that status on your device, so Premium works offline.

Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. We can see aggregated sales reports in the Google Play Console, as all developers can.

5. Device permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of meals or your fridge. Used only when you open the camera.
  • Photos / media: to pick an existing photo from your gallery. Only the photo you select is used.
  • Internet: for the AI features and to check your subscription with Google Play.

You can revoke permissions at any time in your phone's settings. The app keeps working, but the related features won't.

6. This website

calvault.app uses a self-hosted, privacy-focused analytics tool to count visits. It records things like the page viewed, the referring site, browser, operating system, device type and country. It doesn't use advertising cookies or build a profile of you across sites. We use it only to understand which pages are useful.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. The site doesn't have forms, logins or ads.

7. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and help you, and we delete support emails when they're no longer needed.

8. Sharing and selling

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. The only third parties that receive data from the app are the ones described above:

  • our AI service provider, for the AI features you choose to use;
  • Google Play, for purchases and subscription status.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, but since we don't hold your food diary, there's very little we could disclose.

9. Retention and security

Data on your device stays until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Meal photos that are no longer used by a saved meal are cleaned up automatically.

Requests to the AI provider and Google Play are sent over HTTPS. Your on-device data is protected by Android's app sandbox, which keeps other apps from reading it. Use a screen lock to protect your phone.

10. How to delete your data

You're in control, and you don't need to contact us:

  • Delete a single meal: open it in CalVault and tap the delete icon.
  • Delete everything: go to Android Settings → Apps → CalVault → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This permanently removes your profile, meals, photos and history.
  • Cancel Premium: deleting data doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel in the Google Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Because we don't keep your diary on a server, there's no account or server copy to delete. For a support email you sent us, write to support@calvault.app and we'll delete it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it's processed. Since your CalVault data is on your device, you can view, edit and delete it directly in the app. For anything else, email us and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we process data to provide the app you asked for (contract), and with your consent when you choose to use an AI feature or give the app permissions. You can withdraw consent at any time by not using the feature or revoking the permission.

12. Children

CalVault isn't intended for children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we don't knowingly collect data from them. Calorie tracking isn't appropriate for everyone. If you're a parent and have concerns, contact us.

13. Health disclaimer

CalVault provides estimates for general wellness and isn't a medical device. Nothing in the app is medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making major changes to your diet, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or have a history of disordered eating.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change how CalVault handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above. For significant changes, such as adding accounts or cloud sync, we'll also tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
