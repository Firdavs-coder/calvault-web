1. The service

CalVault is a mobile app that helps you log meals, estimate nutrition from photos using AI, track weight and daily activity, and get nutrition suggestions. You don't need an account to use it. You must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country) to use CalVault.

2. Not medical advice

CalVault is a general wellness tool. It's not a medical device and doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

Calorie targets, BMI, meal ideas and chat answers are general information. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet or exercise, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of disordered eating. Never ignore professional advice because of something you saw in the app.

3. AI estimates

Nutrition values from photos, fridge suggestions and chat answers are generated by AI and are estimates. They can be inaccurate or incomplete. For example, they may misjudge portion sizes, miss hidden ingredients or misidentify food. Check results before you rely on them, and edit a meal if something looks off. Don't rely on the app for allergen information.
